ALERT

ALERT, established in 2004 (Registration No. 307/2004), is a South India-based not-for-profit organization headquartered in Vijayawada. Led by YL Jayaraj, who has 47 years of experience in the NGO sector, ALERT specializes in empowering Adivasis in the Fifth Scheduled Areas of Andhra Pradesh. The organization is focused on ensuring the effective implementation of affirmative legislations, securing forest rights, and promoting self-governance and fair compensation for displaced Adivasis.
ALERT partnering with Adivasi Mahasabha, advocating for Adivasi rights and sustainable development supports in availing several thousand acres of individual and community forest rights besides facilitating effective service delivery. It supports natural resource management, fisheries, and the establishment of community-based organizations to enhance livelihoods. ALERT is governed by 35 policies, emphasizing gender justice, women’s empowerment, social inclusion, and sustainability.
